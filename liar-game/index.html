Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 2025
This policy applies to the "AI Liar: Find the Liar" app ("the App"). It explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Account Information: When signing in with Google, your name, email address, and profile picture are collected.
  • Anonymous Login: You may use the app anonymously without a Google account. In this case, no personally identifiable information is collected.
  • Game Data: Data such as energy and premium status required for gameplay is stored on Firebase servers.
  • Local Data: Game records, sound settings, and persona settings are stored only on your device.

2. Automatically Collected Information

The following information may be collected automatically to improve app stability and fix errors.

  • App crash and error logs
  • Device OS version and app version
  • App usage patterns (anonymous statistics)

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide gameplay features and manage accounts
  • To sync energy and premium status
  • To detect errors and improve performance
  • To serve advertisements

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies.

  • Firebase (Google): Account authentication, user data storage, and crash reporting
  • Google AdMob: Banner and rewarded advertisements
  • Google Gemini API: AI player dialogue generation (online mode). In-game messages may be included in API requests.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local data: Game records and settings stored on your device are deleted immediately upon uninstallation.
  • Server data: You may delete your account and all associated server data at any time via Settings → Delete Account within the app. Upon request, your personal data will be deleted without delay.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If this Privacy Policy is updated, we will notify users by updating this page.
